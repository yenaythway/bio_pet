## 🧐 Overview
Discover the breed of any dog or cat instantly. This app uses advanced machine learning to identify pets via your camera or gallery. Because the AI runs **directly on your device**, it works perfectly without an internet connection—ensuring privacy and speed wherever you are.

Whether you're at a shelter, out for a walk, or curious about your own pet, this app provides fast and accurate breed recognition.

## ✨ Key Features
* **🐶 AI Dog Breed Identifier** – Recognize hundreds of dog breeds instantly.
* **🐱 AI Cat Breed Identifier** – Classify cat breeds with high accuracy.
* **🔌 Offline Classification** – No internet? No problem. All processing happens on-device.
* **📸 Camera & Gallery Support** – Scan pets live or upload existing photos.
* **📜 Classification History** – Keep a logged history of all your past results.
* **🌐 Deep Dive** – One-tap access to detailed breed information online via your browser.

## 🛠️ How It Works
1. **Capture:** Point your camera at a pet or select a photo from your gallery.
2. **Process:** The local AI model analyzes the visual features (ears, coat, snout).
3. **Result:** The app displays the most likely breed and saves it to your history.
